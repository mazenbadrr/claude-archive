SOLIDWORKS Modeling Challenge DWG No. Pump-2026-09

Think a soap pump is just a tube and a nozzle? Think again.

  1. 01
    Compound nozzle angles A spout that angles in more than one plane, with a tip that still points where it should.
  2. 02
    Internal fluid channels One clean flow path from the dip tube to the nozzle tip, hidden inside the part.
  3. 03
    Multi-start threads The threaded closure collar that screws the pump onto the bottle.
  4. 04
    Injection-molded drafts Realistic wall thickness, plus drafts that pull from the mold in more than one direction.
