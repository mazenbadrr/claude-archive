QD-RP1-200 Simple Kit

Laser-cut plates and M5 threaded rod turn the McMaster 6475K283 cylinder into a sleeve puller, run from the 100 psi supply through a 24 V pressure-center valve. With both coils off the coupler stays locked; the release coil pulls the sleeve back 5.6 mm and line pressure pushes the socket off the plug. Rev B adds a RELEASED sensor, a mounting tab and a neck shim, and nothing in the frame rubs. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, scrub the timeline.
