3

The propellant lines and both vents use one passive flange-clip design, adapted from Half Cat's HCR-1000 and scaled to 623 psig. Three parts: aluminum male fitting on the vehicle, stainless female on the ground side, and a machined U-clip rated to 250 lbf. PTFE O-ring plus PTFE backup ring. Both mechanisms passed an 80 psi bench test with clean self-ejection.

Quick disconnect · Helium line

Helium fill QD with remote sleeve pull

How it releases

1

A pneumatic cylinder retracts the coupler sleeve.

2

Line pressure pushes the two halves apart.

Design choice

The cylinder reacts against the sleeve tube, not a ground anchor, avoiding the anchor pull-out failures Half Cat saw repeatedly.

Mazen Badr · Dr. Busari's Lab, Morgan State University

4

Helium fill uses the Airmo NuQuip QD30, 15,000 psi, PTFE seal, valved; we have S/N 30163. Release follows the PRQD approach: a pneumatic cylinder pulls the sleeve and line pressure ejects the halves. The cylinder reacts against the sleeve tube instead of a ground anchor, which fixes the failure Half Cat kept seeing. I rebuilt the YJSP Vespula PRQD parametrically in CadQuery with mated and released STEP assemblies.
