7

Thickness was solved, not assumed: 15 in is the least whole-inch pad giving overturning FS of at least 1.5 at 4,000 lbf over every firing angle (worst 1.56 at 15–20 degrees; 14 in fails at 1.48). Dead weight 13,500 lb; passive resistance 2,250 lbf handles sliding. Worst firing angle is 20 degrees above horizontal. A separate fire-protection PWS and quantity-distance analysis went to Procurement and Safety on Sep 11.
